\section*{LLM Usage Statement}
The only LLM tool used in this work is Claude (Anthropic), used as a coding and writing assistant. It helped with
literature searches and source comparison and with feedback on drafts; wrote and ran code for the camera intervention, part of the EFT-S evaluation, the calibration baseline, the
semantic-region analysis, the focal-head fine-tuning (EFT-F), the SR-RAW optical-zoom runs, the focal-shrinkage analysis and its correction (FocalUnshrink),
the additional fine-tuning seeds, the complete-test-set runs, the JPEG-with-zoom control and the supplementary statistics (two-way bootstrap, power-law fit, exponent
intervals, FocalUnshrink sensitivity and its simulation); redrew
Figures 2 and 3 at print size; checked bibliography entries against arXiv, publisher and DOI records; and edited all sections, including moving material
to the supplement. Every number in the paper is generated by a script from the result files. The authors
checked all cited sources, results and claims and are responsible for the submitted version.
