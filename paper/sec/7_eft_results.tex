\section{Results: correcting the models}
\label{sec:eftresults}

\paragraph{Plain self-consistency learns a shortcut.}
EFT lowers $\alpha$ of DA-V2-S from \DavTwoSFixedKittiAlpha{} to \DavTwoSEftKittiAlpha{} on KITTI and
from \DavTwoSFixedNyuAlpha{} to \DavTwoSEftNyuAlpha{} on NYUv2 (\cref{tab:eft}). The blur control reveals how:
the tuned model moves depth by \DavTwoSEftKittiBlur\% on KITTI when the image is only blurred, against
\DavTwoSFixedKittiBlur\% before tuning. It has learned that a blurry image is a zoomed one.

\paragraph{EFT+B removes it.}
With the blur-invariance term the shortcut is gone (\DavTwoSEftbKittiBlur\% on KITTI,
\DavTwoSEftbNyuBlur\% on NYUv2) and $\alpha$ drops further, to \DavTwoSEftbKittiAlpha{} and
\DavTwoSEftbNyuAlpha. On the two datasets never used in training it is \DavTwoSEftbDiodeInAlpha{} and
\DavTwoSEftbDiodeOutAlpha{} (frozen: \DavTwoSFixedDiodeInAlpha{} and \DavTwoSFixedDiodeOutAlpha), so the
model learns part of what a zoom looks like in general.

\paragraph{The cost.}
Equivariance is not free here. AbsRel on unzoomed KITTI images rises from \DavTwoSFixedKittiAbsrelOne{}
to \DavTwoSEftbKittiAbsrelOne{}, and on DIODE-outdoor from \DavTwoSFixedDiodeOutAbsrelOne{} to
\DavTwoSEftbDiodeOutAbsrelOne{}, while NYUv2 improves (\DavTwoSFixedNyuAbsrelOne{} to
\DavTwoSEftbNyuAbsrelOne). A three times stronger anchor does not buy the KITTI accuracy back
(\DavTwoSEftbThreeKittiAbsrelOne) and costs NYUv2 (\DavTwoSEftbThreeNyuAbsrelOne), at the same $\alpha$
(\DavTwoSEftbThreeKittiAlpha, \DavTwoSEftbThreeNyuAlpha); the trade-off is not a matter of tuning the
weight.

\paragraph{What does the work.}
Two controls isolate the ingredients. Blur augmentation alone, without the zoom term, leaves most of the
effect in place ($\alpha$ \DavTwoSBlurKittiAlpha{} on KITTI, \DavTwoSBlurNyuAlpha{} on NYUv2): seeing
low-detail images is not what teaches the model the camera. Replacing the frozen teacher with the
student's own stop-gradient prediction, as in Zhong \etal~\cite{zhong2023equivariance}, reaches a
similar $\alpha$ (\DavTwoSZhongKittiAlpha, \DavTwoSZhongNyuAlpha) but drifts: the blur shortcut returns
(\DavTwoSZhongKittiBlur\% on KITTI) and unzoomed AbsRel degrades most of all variants
(\DavTwoSZhongKittiAbsrelOne{} on KITTI, \DavTwoSZhongNyuAbsrelOne{} on NYUv2). The frozen teacher, the
zoom term and the blur term are each needed.
