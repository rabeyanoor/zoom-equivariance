\section{Results: correcting the models}
\label{sec:eftresults}

\paragraph{Plain self-consistency learns a shortcut.}
EFT lowers $\alpha$ of DA-V2-S from \DavTwoSFixedKittiAlpha{} to \DavTwoSEftKittiAlpha{} on KITTI and
from \DavTwoSFixedNyuAlpha{} to \DavTwoSEftNyuAlpha{} on NYUv2 (\cref{tab:eft}). The blur control reveals how:
the tuned model moves depth by \DavTwoSEftKittiBlur\% on KITTI when the image is only blurred, against
\DavTwoSFixedKittiBlur\% before tuning. It has learned that a blurry image is a zoomed one.

\paragraph{EFT+B removes it.}
With the blur-invariance term the shortcut is gone (\DavTwoSEftbKittiBlur\% on KITTI,
\DavTwoSEftbNyuBlur\% on NYUv2) and $\alpha$ drops further, to \DavTwoSEftbKittiAlpha{} and
\DavTwoSEftbNyuAlpha. On the two datasets never used in training it is \DavTwoSEftbDiodeInAlpha{} and
\DavTwoSEftbDiodeOutAlpha{} (frozen: \DavTwoSFixedDiodeInAlpha{} and \DavTwoSFixedDiodeOutAlpha). Over \eftbSeeds{} training seeds
$\alpha$ is $\EftbSeedsKittiAlphaMean\pm\EftbSeedsKittiAlphaSd$, $\EftbSeedsNyuAlphaMean\pm\EftbSeedsNyuAlphaSd$,
$\EftbSeedsDiodeInAlphaMean\pm\EftbSeedsDiodeInAlphaSd$ and $\EftbSeedsDiodeOutAlphaMean\pm\EftbSeedsDiodeOutAlphaSd$
(mean $\pm$ sd, same order).

\paragraph{The cost.}
AbsRel on unzoomed KITTI images rises from \DavTwoSFixedKittiAbsrelOne{}
to \DavTwoSEftbKittiAbsrelOne{}, and on DIODE-outdoor from \DavTwoSFixedDiodeOutAbsrelOne{} to
\DavTwoSEftbDiodeOutAbsrelOne{} (higher for every seed), while NYUv2 is about unchanged
(\DavTwoSFixedNyuAbsrelOne{} to $\EftbSeedsNyuAbsrelOneMean\pm\EftbSeedsNyuAbsrelOneSd$ over seeds). A stronger
anchor does not buy it back (supplement).

\paragraph{What does the work.}
Blur augmentation alone leaves most of the effect in place ($\alpha$ \DavTwoSBlurKittiAlpha{} on KITTI,
\DavTwoSBlurNyuAlpha{} on NYUv2). The student's own stop-gradient target, as in
Zhong \etal~\cite{zhong2023equivariance}, reaches a similar $\alpha$ (\DavTwoSZhongKittiAlpha,
\DavTwoSZhongNyuAlpha) but drifts: the blur shortcut returns (\DavTwoSZhongKittiBlur\% on KITTI) and unzoomed
AbsRel degrades most (\DavTwoSZhongKittiAbsrelOne{} on KITTI). Teacher, zoom term and blur term are each needed.

\paragraph{Correcting only the scale.}
EFT-S, which learns one number per image, reaches $\alpha=\DavTwoSScaleNyuAlpha$ on NYUv2 at no unzoomed cost
(AbsRel \DavTwoSScaleNyuAbsrelOne, frozen \DavTwoSFixedNyuAbsrelOne). It also transfers better than EFT+B to the unseen
DIODE ($\alpha=\DavTwoSScaleDiodeInAlpha$ and \DavTwoSScaleDiodeOutAlpha, against \DavTwoSEftbDiodeInAlpha{} and
\DavTwoSEftbDiodeOutAlpha), but is weaker on KITTI ($\alpha=\DavTwoSScaleKittiAlpha$) and costs more unzoomed accuracy there and on DIODE-outdoor. Tuning only Depth Pro's focal head (EFT-F)
helps on its training domains, not on DIODE (supplement).
